# V0.6.1 Preservation

V0.6.1 is frozen as the working proof-of-concept generation.

Its historical observer.db must be preserved as experimental evidence and must not be reused or overwritten by V0.7.

V0.7 uses a new database: machineobserver_v07/data/observer-v07.db.

The architectural defect discovered during V0.6.1 testing was active-probe self-contamination: observer-generated probe traffic could re-enter the passive network evidence stream as python.exe traffic. V0.7 therefore makes active probes optional/off by default, labels their output PROBED, and excludes observer-owned probe flows from ordinary passive activity interpretation.
